Sexual Assault Forensic Examination (SAFE) Program
If you have experienced sexual violence/assault and wish to seek medical care, the SAFE Program is available at each hospital in the Interior Health Region. This type of care can offer STI prevention, care, and treatment as well as emergency contraceptive options. There is no obligation to file a police report to access this service. You are welcome to bring a support individual such as a friend or case worker.

UBC Okanagan Psychology Clinic
The Psychology Clinic serves as a training centre for graduate students studying clinical psychology at UBC Okanagan. Student clinicians are always supervised by registered clinical psychologists and neuropsychologists. Provides low-cost access to psychology services in areas including anxiety disorders, depression, learning disability and psychoeducational assessment, neuropsychological assessment, OCD, PTSD, psychotherapy, substance use, and wellness. The clinic is not able to offer crisis or emergency services. Eligibility and fees are determined individually and discussed with each client or referral source. Open to the general public.

Hours: 8:00am-4:00pm Monday-Friday. Closed statutory holidays, campus reading breaks, and July-August for summer break.
Contact: 250-807-8241, ipc.ok@ubc.ca
Address: Okanagan Campus, Department of Psychology, 1147 Research Road, ART 323, Kelowna
Website: psych.ok.ubc.ca/psychology-clinic
