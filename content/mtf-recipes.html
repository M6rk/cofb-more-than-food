Chickpea Greek Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • oregano, salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Dice the pepper and cucumber and thinly slice the red onion.
  2. Add the chickpeas, pepper, cucumber, and red onion to a large bowl.
  3. Whisk together the mustard, olive oil, lemon juice, honey, oregano, salt and pepper.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and gently mix until everything is well coated.
  5. Tip: If you want it to be more filling, add quinoa, grilled chicken, or toasted pita.

Easy Zucchini Bread

Ingredients

  • 1 & ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • S½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups grated zucchini

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350F and grease a loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, toss together the flour, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the oil, sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  4. Stir in the grated zucchini.
  5. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients. Stir until just combined. Spoon batter into the prepared baking pan.
  6. Bake for 60-65 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 1 & ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 & 1/2 cups (340g) overripe mashed bananas, (about 3 large or 4 medium)
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325F and grease a loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, toss together the flour, sugar, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the mashed bananas, oil, eggs, and vanilla. Add to the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  4. Bake for about 60 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
