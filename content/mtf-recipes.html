Hearty Vegetable Soup

A warm, filling soup that stretches canned and fresh vegetables into a full meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes
  • 1 can beans, drained
  • 4 cups broth or water
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and carrots, cook 5 minutes until soft.
  3. Add tomatoes, beans, and broth. Bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, then serve.

Bean & Rice Bowl

A simple, protein-packed bowl that comes together with just a few pantry staples.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice, uncooked
  • 1 can beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: salsa or shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Warm beans in a small pot with cumin and garlic powder.
  3. Spoon beans over rice.
  4. Top with salsa or cheese if desired, then serve.

Overnight Oats

A make-ahead breakfast that needs no cooking and keeps in the fridge for days.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1/2 cup milk (or water)
  • 1 tbsp honey or sugar, optional
  • Fruit, for topping

Instructions

  1. Combine oats, milk, and sweetener in a jar or container.
  2. Stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
  3. In the morning, stir again and top with fruit.

Sheet Pan Chicken & Veggies

One pan, minimal cleanup, and an easy way to use whatever vegetables are on hand.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs or breasts
  • 3 cups mixed vegetables, chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss chicken and vegetables with oil and seasonings on a sheet pan.
  3. Spread evenly and roast 25–30 minutes, until chicken is cooked through.

No-Bake Energy Bites

A simple no-cook snack, great for kids or an easy grab-and-go bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup oats
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • Optional: raisins or chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until well combined.
  2. Roll into small balls, about 1 inch wide.
  3. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes before serving.
